THE REGGIO EMILIA SENSORY SANCTUARY

Respecting the Pace of Early Childhood

The toddler years are a precious window of rapid neurological formation. In our Toddler Discoverers atelier, children are not hurried. Instead, natural wood materials, open daylight, water ripples, and acoustic singing invite natural language and emotional resilience.

Gentle Separation

Comforting dropoff rituals where caregivers bond closely with every family.

Tactile Water & Clay

Safe food-grade sensory materials that encourage grip and sensory discovery.

French Rhymes

Early dual-language exposure embedded naturally into lullabies and meal games.

Chef Organic Puree

Scratch-cooked purees from fresh local farms promoting healthy feeding.

Predictable, Joyful Routine

Daily Timetable • Toddler Discoverers

Warm Welcome & Sensory Stations

Reggio Music & Fingerplay Circle

Acoustic songs, nursery rhymes, mirror movement, and bilingual sound play.

Tactile Messy Atelier: Water & Clay

10:45 AM

Organic Chef Puree & Hydration

Turf Grass Outdoor Play & Sensory Walk

12:15 PM

Lullaby Story Circle & Departure / Nap

Developmental Goals

Core Milestones & Learning Rubrics

Qualitative milestones documented daily and synchronized to your Parent Portal Dashboard.

Emergent Language

Independent self-feeding initiation, hand washing rituals, and sensory hygiene.
