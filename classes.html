Homework Philosophy 01

What is your homework policy for Kindergarten and Primary School?

We strictly reject nightly repetitive worksheets and rote drill memorization. Cognitive research demonstrates that after-school fatigue impairs deep neuro-consolidation. Instead, we prescribe joyful “Inquiry Extensions”: reading high-interest literature together, baking to practice fractions, or collecting nature specimens.

Zero Drill Sheets • 100% Real-World Inquiry
Differentiation 02

How do educators differentiate for gifted learners or those needing more support?

With our 1:8 ratio, instruction is personalized in real time. Children showing rapid mastery enter higher-order Socratic challenges and robotics blocks, while those needing support explore concepts with tactile manipulatives.

1:8 Mentor-Child Ratio
Digital Wellness 03

What is LumiKids’ classroom screen time and technology policy?

100% screen-free policy for ages 1.5–4. In primary grades, screens are strictly active creative instruments (MIT ScratchJr coding, stop-motion animation), capped at 30 mindful minutes weekly.

100% Screen-Free Ages 1.5–4
Graduate Outcomes 04

How successfully do graduates transition into competitive middle schools?

Our Grade 5 graduates hold an unmatched 100% acceptance rate into top-choice independent prep academies and competitive magnet schools, praised for articulate confidence and inquiry grit.

100% Top-Choice Acceptance
Assessment 05

How do parents stay informed on academic progress without traditional letter grades?

Via our encrypted real-time Parent Portal with photo observations and qualitative growth rubrics, plus a 45-minute semester symposium where parents review their child’s growth dossier.

Encrypted Live Parent Portal
