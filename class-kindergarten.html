Socratic Inquiry & Multisensory STEAM

At age 3 to 5, children shift from solitary sensory discoveries to vibrant collaborative problem-solving. Our Kindergarten Explorers immerse in concrete mathematics with Singapore Math counters, decode early literature through Jolly Phonics, and test physical hypotheses in the maker atelier.

Jolly Phonics Immersion

42 letter sounds, multi-sensory blending, and storybook reading circles.

Singapore Math Framework

Concrete manipulatives, number bonds, and spatial geometry pattern recognition.

Screen-Free Robotics

Coding caterpillars, gear balance trials, and foundational algorithmic thinking.

Botanical Greenhouse

Hands-on planting, insect habitat observation, and weather logging.
