Our Origin Story

From a Modest Cottage to a 5-Acre Eco-Living Sanctuary

LumiKids began in 2011 when a team of passionate developmental psychologists and early childhood educators realized that modern education was squeezing spontaneous wonder out of young learners.

We started with just 18 toddlers in a sunlit garden cottage. Today, on our lush 5-acre eco-campus, over 2,400 alumni have graduated with a fierce passion for inquiry, a deep reverence for nature, and unbreakable empathy.

Our Compass & Purpose

Vision, Mission & Guiding Principles

Every lesson, interaction, and playground moment is anchored in these four sacred commitments.

Our Vision

To Nurture Kind Hearts, Inquiring Minds & Fearless Creators

We envision a world where every young person steps into adulthood with unbroken self-esteem, deep scientific fluency, compassionate leadership, and a lifelong thirst to make their communities better.

Our Mission

Merging Playful Discovery with Rigorous Experiential Learning

Our mission is to provide an emotionally secure, inquiry-rich environment where Reggio Emilia exploration, hands-on STEAM innovation, biophilic outdoor immersion, and transparent parent partnership flourish hand-in-hand.

Bold Curiosity

No question is too silly. We encourage children to hypothesize, test, stumble, and celebrate the thrilling eureka moment of discovery.

Radical Kindness

Emotional intelligence is as vital as literacy. We teach deep listening, conflict resolution with grace, and rejoicing in others' victories.

Eco-Stewardship

We don't just teach environmentalism from books; our children compost, cultivate heirloom seedlings, and preserve biodiversity on campus.

Authentic Voice

Every child possesses a unique 100 Languages of Expression — through painting, storytelling, building, code, music, and quiet reflection.

A Legacy of Laughter & Discovery

Our 15-Year Milestone Journey

How a simple dream blossomed into the region's benchmark for experiential early childhood education.

2011
The Humble Beginning

The Sunshine Cottage Opens

Dr. Evelyn Vance opens LumiKids with 18 eager toddlers and a revolutionary play-based philosophy that prioritized questions over rote memorization.

2015
Campus Expansion

Inauguration of the 5-Acre Eco-Sanctuary

Relocated to Cambridge Meadows, expanding into a biophilic nature campus with barefoot trails, sensory gardens, and an open-air amphitheater.

2019
Technological Wonder

Launch of the Young Inventors STEAM Pavilion

Integrated tactile coding blocks, solar windmill laboratories, and 3D geometric construction into the daily kindergarten and primary curriculum.

2026
LumiKids 2.0 Digital Era

Next-Gen Connected Parent Portal & Global Recognition

Voted "Most Joyful Early Learning Academy" and launched real-time developmental parent reporting, live attendance tracking, and international bilingual programs.
